Layer by Layer Daily Tools

A private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Layer by Layer Daily to manage that one channel: upload its own Shorts with a scheduled publish time, read the statistics of its own videos, and read and reply to comments on its own videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
