Privacy Policy

The tool reads only the owner's own TikTok profile info and public video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) through the official TikTok API, with the owner's permission. The data is stored privately on the owner's computer to plan content. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions).
